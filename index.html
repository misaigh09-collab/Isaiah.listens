FAQ

Good questions

Is this therapy?

No. This is a paid listening and emotional-support service. It is not medical, psychological or counselling care. In a crisis or emergency, please contact a qualified professional or your local emergency services.

How do I pay?

Fees are paid in advance, before the session begins. Message me to confirm the payment details for your package.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, with at least 12 hours’ notice. Late cancellations forfeit 50% of the fee.

Is what I share really private?

Yes. Nothing you share is repeated, screenshotted, posted or gossiped about. The only exceptions are where disclosure is required by law or to protect someone from serious harm.

What happens if feelings are caught?

One warning is issued. If it continues, the penalty in Clause 6 applies. The Listener’s decision is final, binding and not open to debate, tears or flowers.
